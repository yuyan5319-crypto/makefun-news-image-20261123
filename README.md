# The World in One Day — 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026, as a
dark newsroom front page with a globe and six stories from around the
world:

1. **Climate — Antalya, Turkey:** COP31 talks run past their deadline;
   the EU refuses to endorse the draft deal.
2. **Energy — Strait of Hormuz:** two oil tankers struck by projectiles
   as Iran–US tensions rise.
3. **Disaster — Peru:** a magnitude 6.7 earthquake hits the Andes in
   central Peru.
4. **Disaster — Indonesia:** a factory fire in Banten kills three
   workers; 36 are missing.
5. **War & diplomacy — Ukraine:** Trump presses a wider Ukraine
   ceasefire as a drone strike near Kyiv leaves 14 missing.
6. **Space — Mercury:** the BepiColombo probe reaches Mercury.

The image is drawn by hand (flat pictograms and typeset headlines, plus
a generated globe illustration in the header) — no news photographs.
`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; run it locally
with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`.
